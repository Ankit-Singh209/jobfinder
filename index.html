Before you apply

  • Genuine employers and agencies never ask you to pay for an interview, offer letter, or "registration."
  • Apply through the official link shown here, or the company's own careers page — not a forwarded PDF or WhatsApp form.
  • A real recruiter's email matches the company's domain (e.g. @company.com), not a free Gmail/Yahoo address.

Semiconductor, VLSI & Networking Hardware — career pages

VLSI/chip-design and core networking-hardware hiring is concentrated at these employers, and almost none of them expose a public job API — so unlike the live search above, these are direct links you browse yourself.

Big MNCs & IT majors — career pages

These employ the largest numbers of freshers and professionals in India but run their own hiring systems (Workday, SuccessFactors, custom portals) without a public API, so they're not in the live search above — go straight to their pages.
